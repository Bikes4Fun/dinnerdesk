# Privacy policy

<!-- Written by scripts/write_privacy_md.py from copy.json. Edit copy.json, not this file. -->

Dinner Desk keeps only what it needs to plan your household's meals. Here's the short version.

## Agreeing to this policy

The first time you open Dinner Desk without an account, you see a short summary of this policy and continue by agreeing to it. Creating an account asks you to tick a box agreeing to it. We store which version you agreed to and when, tied to your account or to your guest kitchen (not your IP address). If this policy changes in a way that matters, we show the summary again.

## Account

If you create an account we store your email (or the username you chose instead, plus a recovery email if you add one), whether and when you confirmed your email, a hash of your password (not the password), and your household name. You stay signed in for 30 days. You can sign out of this device, or every device, from Account & security.

Forgot-password emails are sent by Resend. We store a hash of the reset link, not the link. The link works once, for one hour. We answer the same way whether or not that email or username has an account (or a recovery email). You can change your email, swap it for a username, or add, change or remove a recovery email from Account & security; a new email only takes over once you open the confirm link we send it.

Confirm-email emails are also sent by Resend. While a confirm link is open we store the address it was sent to and a hash of the link, not the link. The link works once, for 24 hours.

If you subscribe to the email newsletter, we store that choice with your household and may email you Dinner Desk Deals. Turn it off anytime in Settings.

## Your kitchen

Plans, grocery lists, pantry, recipe edits, favorites, hides, to-try marks, filters, and store or aisle choices are stored for your household in a database we host. Taste Lab stores the meals you like or pass, plus diet, allergy, and dislike choices. When you are signed in, those answers are tied to your account and used to suggest meals. Thumbs up or down on a planned meal, and on a prep step, are stored for your household and used to choose future meal suggestions. Prep step votes are kept so we can improve prep suggestions.

Someone you invite joins that same household and can see and change it, including its filters and allergies, and can see everyone's meal likes and dislikes and Taste Lab history. Invite links expire after 7 days. You can remove a member from Account & security. Removing them ends their access.

## Without an account

Guests use a private kitchen identified by a random device or browser cookie. All normal features work without an account. Reinstalling the app or clearing browser data can lose access to guest data. Create an account to protect it, or delete the kitchen from our server under Your data on this page.

## What we don't collect

We don't sell or share your data. If that ever changes, we'll tell you first. We don't take payment or location data, and we don't look at your contacts or other apps. We don't connect to health apps or medical records; the only health-related information we keep is the allergies and diets you choose to enter, used only to filter your meals. Nutrition-tracker sync is not built. We don't run ads or analytics.

Recipe photos in the app are ones we have marked as ours to use. We don't collect photos, reviews, or comments from cooks yet.

The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so that request goes to Google. The apps don't.

## Deleting data

Download a copy of everything stored for your household (one file; no passwords or sign-in tokens) under Your data on this page. Delete your account from Account & security after confirming your password. Your sign-ins, reset links and confirm links are removed. If other members remain, a household creator must transfer ownership before deleting their account. The last member's deletion also removes their solo kitchen. The household creator or a household admin can separately delete a household: its kitchen and plans are removed, but members keep their accounts and get empty solo households. Leaving or being removed from a household also keeps your account.

Without deleting anything else, you can clear your Taste Lab history, and the household creator or an admin can clear the household's meal ratings or prep votes, under Clear part of your data on this page. Meal votes, cleared Taste Lab history and cleared meal ratings may be kept without any account, household, browser or session identifiers.

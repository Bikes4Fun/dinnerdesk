Variant review

Accept: the right side is a variant of the left (hidden by default when the left one fits a household). Separate: a different meal; leave both alone. Swap: same meal, but the right side should be the main one. Click a card, then press A / S / W to mark it (it stays put so you can look it over); J / K move between cards. Click the same choice again to undo it. Choices save in this browser as you go; Export writes them to a file.
